# Assumptions

## User and behavior assumptions

- Users will provide photos faster than they will complete forms.
- Carers are willing to pay for reliability and peace of mind.
- Users will trust AI answers if responses are grounded and cite source records.

## Product assumptions

- Model number alone is enough to unlock useful manual/troubleshooting answers in most cases.
- Event-log timeline creates ongoing retention through utility and accountability.
- Appliance-first entry point can expand naturally into subscriptions/utilities management.

## Commercial assumptions

- Commission and referral models can monetize before broad subscription conversion.
- UK-first launch provides enough market size to validate product-market fit.
- Adjacent competitor tools (Notion + finance apps) leave a workflow gap worth paying to close.

## Technology assumptions

- Serverless architecture can support MVP without unacceptable latency/cost.
- Open Banking integration via TrueLayer is feasible early in roadmap and improves retention.
