# Fieldset recommendation candidates

The [registry seed](../../../server/src/db/seeds/fieldset-recommendations.ts) contains 46 task candidates and 41 purchasable candidates across 53 fieldsets. Import and Refresh recommendations pass candidates from the Thing’s selected fieldsets to the recommendation models.

Eligibility describes the feature, compatibility or terms that make a candidate applicable. Use the Thing’s manual, contract or policy to establish timing and procedure. Optional importance and plain-text recurrence estimates, such as “every month”, guide the task model. Purchasable candidates can include a link to a listing.

Use UK wording and processes where they apply. Confirm the Thing’s jurisdiction before suggesting a statutory task. The [source register](sources.md#fieldset-recommendations) covers examples of model care and UK processes.
